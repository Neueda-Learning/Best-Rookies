portfolio_manager

Portfolio Manager is an investment portfolio management system. It supports asset entry, return tracking, and position analysis, helping users manage stocks, funds, and other investment products in one place while clearly viewing profit, loss, and diversification risk.

Core Capabilities

  • Asset entry and centralized management for stocks, funds, and other investment products.
  • Return statistics and profit-loss tracking for quick performance review.
  • Position analysis and risk diversification views to support investment decisions.
